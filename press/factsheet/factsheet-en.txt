[Long description]
VERMILION: Three Kingdoms is a collector RPG for iPhone and iPad. Gather more than 100 generals drawn as realistic portraits, each with unique skills and a biography. Bonds between generals unlock hidden power, and formations and tactics carry you through a 50-battle campaign spanning the Three Kingdoms era, with branching endings shaped by your choices. In the action mode One vs. Ten Thousand you control a single general and cut through an army (runs of about 2.5 minutes, 7 battlefields, difficulty tiers). A codex and practice-yard mini games (duel, quiz) round it out. There are no ads and no subscriptions, and recruiting uses only resources earned through play — no paid random draws. Optional growth items exist, but every general can be obtained by playing. No account or server: the game runs fully offline. Download for free and play the first 12 battlefields (up to Guandu); a one-time purchase unlocks the main campaign.
